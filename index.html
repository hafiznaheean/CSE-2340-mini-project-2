A cup with a story

It started with one kettle

In 2014, we set up a kettle and three stools outside the New Market bus stand. Office workers and students kept coming back, and the stall slowly became the tea house you see today.

A tea stall that grew into a tea house

We still buy leaves directly from small gardens and brew every cup to order.

Direct from the garden

We work with 6 small growers in Sylhet and Panchagarh, so you taste the leaf and not the middleman.

Brewed to order

No big flasks sitting for hours. Each pot is steeped for the time its leaf needs, between 3 and 5 minutes.

Local, always

Our snacks come from family recipes and our sweets from two neighbourhood makers in Chattogram.

What regulars say

Good to know

Do you take reservations?+

Yes. Use the booking form below for groups of up to 6. Smaller groups are welcome to walk in.

Is there vegetarian food?+

Most of our snacks and all of our sweets are vegetarian. The Beef Roll and Chicken Samosa are the only meat items.

Can I buy tea leaves to take home?+

Yes. All 24 blends are sold by weight at the counter, packed fresh each morning.

Is there Wi-Fi and space to study?+

There is free Wi-Fi and plenty of seats upstairs. Please keep the tables free during the 5 pm rush.
